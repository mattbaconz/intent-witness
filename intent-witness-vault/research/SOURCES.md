## Agent-skill ecosystem research

### GITSKILLS-2026
- ArXiv: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10906`
- GitSkills reports millions of SKILL.md occurrences across public repositories in a July 2026 collection. Used as evidence that skill files themselves are massively commoditized and not a moat.

## Research hygiene

- Re-check all volatile current-feature claims before public launch.
- Prefer official docs/repositories for technical capability claims.
- Do not use GitHub star counts as timeless evidence; if quoted, record exact retrieval date.
- Do not copy competitor prompt text into Deliberate.
