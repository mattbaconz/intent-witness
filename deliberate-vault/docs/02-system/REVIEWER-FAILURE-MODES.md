# Reviewer Failure Modes

## F1 — Vague designer-speak

Symptoms:

- “improve hierarchy”;
- “create breathing room”;
- “make it premium”;
- “add delight.”

Mitigation: evidence + product mismatch + depth are required fields.

## F2 — Anti-card dogma

Reviewer counts surfaces and condemns them independent of semantics.

Mitigation: require explanation of what enclosure does to grouping/hierarchy. Counterexample fixtures where cards are correct.

## F3 — Anti-AI minimalism

Reviewer reacts to performative AI tropes by making everything grayscale, sparse, and square.

Mitigation: expressive/justified fixtures; project design language; measure output diversity.

## F4 — Novelty bias

Reviewer rewards unusual structures even when familiar conventions are faster to understand.

Mitigation: user-job clarity outranks novelty; convention checklist.

## F5 — Builder anchoring

Reviewer receives too much implementation rationale and accepts choices because they were already made.

Mitigation: fresh context, product intent first, read-only review, limit builder rationale.

## F6 — Requirement hallucination

Reviewer invents a persona/workflow not in product context.

Mitigation: uncertainty, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, explicit intent source.

## F7 — Shallow repair

Reviewer correctly identifies a deep issue but proposes “reduce padding and borders.”

Mitigation: problem depth + revision constraint; alternatives requirement.

## F8 — Over-review

Strong existing design is rewritten to show activity.

Mitigation: PASS is a successful outcome; fixture where no redesign is needed.

## F9 — Endless contradiction

One iteration removes grouping; next demands more grouping.

Mitigation: comparison review, original finding IDs, max cycles, decision memory.

## F10 — Screenshot tunnel vision

Reviewer optimizes static appearance while missing interaction path/empty/error/mobile states.

Mitigation: interaction and responsive evidence when relevant; deep-review mode.

## F11 — Source-code tunnel vision

Reviewer flags component names/style usage without verifying rendered effect.

Mitigation: rendered evidence required for material visual claims.

## F12 — Fake measurement authority

Reviewer presents subjective heuristics as calibrated numerical truth.

Mitigation: no global score in V0; qualitative/ordinal confidence.

## F13 — Reference cloning

Reviewer turns references into literal theme recipes.

Mitigation: principle extraction; anti-reference process; popular-reference eval cases.

## F14 — Page-content prompt injection

Rendered UI text tells reviewer to ignore instructions.

Mitigation: page content is untrusted data.
